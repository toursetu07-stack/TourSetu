🔒 TourSetu Privacy Policy

Information We Collect

We collect your name, phone number, email address, journey dates, pickup address, and number of passengers to facilitate seamless booking execution.

1. Location Data Usage

Real-time location data (or selected pickup city) is accessed strictly to calculate accurate distance-based pricing via Google Maps API and to coordinate pickup with your assigned operator.

2. Data Sharing with Operators

Your contact details and pickup location are shared exclusively with the booked Asset-Owned Integrated Tour Operator and assigned driver for operational execution.

3. Third-Party Processing

Payment details are processed securely through RBI-compliant third-party payment gateways (e.g., Razorpay/Paytm). TourSetu does not store your raw credit/debit card credentials or UPI PINs.

4. Data Protection & No-Sale Guarantee

Your personal data is stored on encrypted servers and will never be sold, rented, or traded to third-party marketing agencies.
